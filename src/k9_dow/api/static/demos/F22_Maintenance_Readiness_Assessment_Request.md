### **Title:**

**Request for Support: F-22 Raptor Aircraft Maintenance & Mission Readiness Enhancement**

### **Document Body:**

The 325th Maintenance Squadron requests an architectural assessment and recommendation package to improve the F-22 Raptor’s maintenance workflow, subsystem health monitoring, and weapons load validation processes. Recent training simulations have identified recurring issues in aircraft diagnostic accuracy, electronic warfare suite checks, and weapons interface integrity, especially during high-tempo mission scenarios.

During pre-flight operations, maintenance crews reported inconsistent hydraulic system readings, delays in subsystem initialization, and incomplete data transfer from the Mission Data File (MDF) loader to the onboard avionics package. Additionally, technicians highlighted a lack of unified digital traceability across Maintenance_Event records, making it difficult to correlate subsystem failures to mission profiles.

Weapons flights also observed occasional anomalies in weapons rail verification, particularly around simulated MIL-STD-1760 compliance checks and torque validation steps when loading AIM-120 training rounds.

The Wing Commander has requested a more integrated, predictive, and digitally traceable approach to F-22 maintenance and mission readiness to support upcoming synthetic training rotations. The assessment should include:

1. Identification of capability gaps in the maintenance and diagnostics workflow.
2. Recommended improvements to operational activities supporting aircraft readiness.
3. Assessment of existing system interfaces across the radar, EW suite, Vehicle Management System (VMS), Power Thermal Management System (PTMS), and the Stores Management System (SMS).
4. Suggested enhancements to data integration between the Maintenance Control System (MCS), System Health Monitoring service, and mission planning subsystems.
5. DOTMLPF-P analysis focusing on doctrine, training, personnel skills, and facility constraints relevant to F-22 support conditions.
6. A synthesized architectural view (OV/SV/CV) to support decision-making for modernization programs.

The intent is to ensure the maintenance pipeline becomes more predictive, data-driven, and aligned with future synthetic missions such as Raptor-Next-25 initiative.

Please provide a structured architectural review with traceability to capabilities, operational activities, systems, services, data entities, and relevant standards.
