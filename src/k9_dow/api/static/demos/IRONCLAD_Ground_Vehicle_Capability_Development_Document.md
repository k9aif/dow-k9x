# CAPABILITY DEVELOPMENT DOCUMENT (CDD) FOR IRONCLAD INCREMENT 1

**Document Status:** Final / Unclassified
**Validation Authority:** Army Requirements Oversight Council
**Milestone Decision Authority:** Army Acquisition Executive

## 1. Capability Discussion

This program addresses the capability gaps identified in the Initial
Capabilities Document (ICD) for Survivable Infantry Transport in
Near-Peer Contested Terrain. Current wheeled infantry carriers lack
sufficient protection against top-attack munitions and underbelly
blast threats now proliferated to near-peer and proxy forces, while
remaining light enough for air and rail transportability standards
required for rapid theater deployment.

## 2. Concept of Operations (CONOPS) Summary

- **Primary Mission:** Transport a dismounted infantry squad under
  armor to the point of dismount, providing suppressive fire support
  during approach and withdrawal.
- **Transportability:** Must be transportable by C-17 (two vehicles
  per aircraft) and by rail on standard flatcars without exceeding
  host-nation bridge and tunnel clearance limits in the primary
  theaters of employment.
- **Crew and Capacity:** Crew of 2 (driver, gunner) plus a
  9-soldier dismount squad with full combat load.

## 3. Program Summary

- **Acquisition Objective:** Total of 480 baseline vehicles.
  - *Active Force:* 360 vehicles
  - *National Guard:* 90 vehicles
  - *War Reserve stock:* 30 vehicles
- **System Composition:** Base armored hull with a remote weapon
  station, integrated active protection system, and a
  driver-assist sensor suite for degraded-visibility operation.
- **Planned Service Life:** 25 Years, consistent with prior combat
  vehicle program service life expectations.

## 4. System Capabilities & Performance Parameters

Requirements are structured using Thresholds (T) — the minimum
acceptable operational value — and Objectives (O) — the desired
optimal capability.

| Key Performance Parameter (KPP) / Attribute | Performance Threshold (T) | Performance Objective (O) |
|---|---|---|
| **KPP 1: Survivability** | Defeat top-attack munitions equivalent to current-generation shoulder-fired threats at the vehicle's frontal and top armor arcs. | Defeat top-attack munitions equivalent to current-generation shoulder-fired threats across all armor arcs. |
| **KPP 2: Transportability** | Combat-loaded weight not to exceed C-17 payload limits for two vehicles per sortie. | Combat-loaded weight not to exceed C-130 payload limits for one vehicle per sortie. |
| **KPP 3: Mobility** | Sustained road speed of 65 mph and cross-country speed of 30 mph on trafficable terrain. | Sustained road speed of 70 mph and cross-country speed of 40 mph on trafficable terrain. |
| **KPP 4: Reliability** | Mean Miles Between Operational Mission Failure (MMBOMF) of >= 1,200 miles. | Mean Miles Between Operational Mission Failure (MMBOMF) of >= 2,000 miles. |
| **Attribute 5: Sensor Suite** | Driver-assist sensors provide usable situational awareness in zero-illumination and moderate obscurant conditions. | Driver-assist sensors provide usable situational awareness in zero-illumination and heavy obscurant (smoke, dust) conditions. |

## 5. Other DOTMLPF-P & Supportability Considerations

- **Facilities:** Vehicle must fit within existing motor pool
  maintenance bay dimensions at 90% of current continental United
  States (CONUS) installations without facility modification.
- **Training:** Crew qualification course length must not exceed the
  current wheeled infantry carrier program of instruction by more
  than 2 weeks, to avoid a training-base capacity increase.
- **Sustainment Commonality:** Powertrain and running-gear components
  should maximize parts commonality with existing fielded combat
  vehicle fleets to limit new sustainment supply chain establishment.

## 6. Program Affordability & Life-Cycle Cost

- **Target Unit Procurement Cost:** Shall not exceed $6.2M per vehicle
  in FY2026 dollars.
- **Sustainment Cost Cap:** Operating and support costs must remain
  under $180 per mile over the 25-year service life.
