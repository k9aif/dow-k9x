Identify Stakeholders

How to Identify Stakeholders
Where to Look for Stakeholders in RFPs/SOWs
* Look for phrases like "key personnel," "project team," "users," "operators," or "end users"
* Note any mentions of oversight bodies, review boards, or approval authorities
* Identify organizational charts, reporting relationships, or command structures
* Analyze descriptions of existing processes to identify who performs current functions
* Look for words indicating involvement: "coordinate with," "report to," "interface with," "support"
* Note regulatory bodies, compliance organizations, or standards groups mentioned
* Stakeholders often appear in project governance and management sections
* If they mention "user acceptance," identify who the users are
* "Training requirements" indicates operators, maintainers, and trainers as stakeholders
* Contract requirements often specify government and contractor stakeholder roles
* Technical specifications may reveal system integrators, suppliers, or vendors
* Security requirements suggest cybersecurity personnel and accreditation authorities
* Performance metrics often correspond to stakeholder success criteria
* If "operational effectiveness" is measured, identify the operational community
Key Indicators and Language Patterns
* "The user community includes..."
* "Key stakeholders are..."
* "Project team consists of..."
* "Reporting structure..."
* "Coordinate with," "interface with," "work closely with"
* "Approve," "review," "oversee," "manage"
* "Operate," "maintain," "support," "train"
* "Comply with," "meet requirements of" (indicates regulatory stakeholders)

Instructions:
* Examine the provided text, and identify all individuals, groups, or organizations that have interests, influence, or involvement in the project
* Examine the text multiple times to ensure that you have not missed any stakeholders
* Be extremely verbose and comprehensive in your descriptions, providing citations to the original text
* Consider both explicit mentions and implicit references to stakeholder roles

Mandatory Output Requirements:
Utilize the following format for each stakeholder, with bullets for the description and citation, and an empty line between each stakeholder:
Stakeholder # : 1 complete sentence identifying the stakeholder and their primary role
* Description: From the perspective of the project, generate a complete description of the stakeholder's interests, influence, and involvement. Include their responsibilities, decision-making authority, and impact on project success. Be comprehensive in your description with a target of 100-250 words, with no penalty for word count.
* Citation: Include a citation back to source content to make it easy to understand why we believe this is a stakeholder. If there are multiple documents or sections, then include the document or section name in the citation, along with the original text.
Expected Artifact Output:
#Stakeholder Identification Report
Section 1: Executive Summary
* Summarize the stakeholders, providing common themes as identified
* Use between 500 to 750 words in the summary
* Each paragraph must have a minimum of 5 to 6 sentences
* Be sure to provide the total number of identified stakeholders and double check the number at the end
Section 2: Identified Stakeholders
Section 3: Stakeholder Analysis Matrix
* Organize stakeholders by influence level (high, medium, low)
* Categorize by interest level (high, medium, low)
* Identify primary vs. secondary stakeholders
* Note key relationships and dependencies
Section 4: Glossary of Terms

Quality Assurance Standards:
DO NOT MAKE UP FACTS EVER. If you don't know the answer, state so. Structure your findings to directly inform:
* Stakeholder engagement strategy based on influence and interest
* Communication planning considering military hierarchy
* Risk assessment from stakeholder conflicts or misalignment
* Change management considering stakeholder buy-in requirements
* Project governance reflecting stakeholder decision-making authority
