Identify Constraints
 
How to Identify Constraints
1. Stakeholder Analysis
    - Primary Stakeholders: End users, operators, maintainers
    - Secondary Stakeholders: Regulators, oversight bodies, funding organizations
    - Method: Conduct structured interviews, surveys, and workshops to understand each stakeholder's non-negotiable requirements
2. Regulatory and Policy Review
    - DoD Directives and Instructions
    - Federal regulations (FAR, DFARS)
    - Industry standards (ISO, IEEE, NIST)
    - Security policies (RMF, FISMA)
    - Method: Systematic review of applicable governance documents
3. Operational Environment Analysis
    - Physical constraints: Size, weight, power, cooling
    - Environmental conditions: Temperature, humidity, shock, vibration
    - Deployment constraints: Geographic locations, infrastructure limitations
    - Method: Site surveys, operational assessments, environmental studies
4. Resource Constraints Assessment
    - Budget limitations: Development, procurement, lifecycle costs
    - Schedule constraints: Delivery deadlines, milestone requirements
    - Personnel limitations: Available expertise, training requirements
    - Technology maturity: Available vs. desired technology readiness levels
5. Interface and Integration Analysis
    - Legacy system compatibility
    - Data format requirements
    - Communication protocols
    - Interoperability mandates
    - Method: Architecture analysis, system interface documentation review

Constraint Categories to Examine
1. Technical Constraints
    - Performance requirements (speed, accuracy, throughput)
    - Reliability and availability requirements
    - Security classification levels
    - Technology refresh cycles
2. Operational Constraints
    - Mission requirements and operational concepts (CONOPS)
    - Training and skill level requirements
    - Maintenance and logistics limitations
    - Deployment timelines
3. Business/Programmatic Constraints
    - Funding profiles and budget ceilings
    - Contract terms and conditions
    - Intellectual property restrictions
    - Vendor/supplier limitations
4. Regulatory/Compliance Constraints
    - Safety certifications required
    - Environmental compliance (NEPA)
    - Export control regulations (ITAR, EAR)
    - Cybersecurity frameworks

- Clear statement, that can stand on its own, of what is constrained
