## **Additional Operational Notes**

* IBS is a **maintenance + sustainment** contract; new work is add-ons.
* They use **Navy ERP**, but limited functionality meets MSC needs.
* Many custom and homegrown applications exist.
* Shipboard logistics applications are part of MSC’s workload.
* MSC serves other customers such as **DDGs** and **NOAA**.

### **DevSecOps & Cloud Issues**

* Mission owners manage their own DevSecOps pipelines.
* CDIDC pipeline:
  * **Not mature**, inconsistent, some breaks.
* Attempted USAF Cloud One:
  * Security issues
  * Did not proceed
* MSC is under **Fleet Forces** and **TRANSCOM** (Echelon II).
* Navy Cloud Broker model was fumbled:
  * NAVAIR → did their own way
  * DC2HS → their own way
  * NAVSUP → their own way
* Cloud costs rising:
  * 20% increase this year
  * 25% last year
* Cannot easily switch cloud provider → less pricing leverage.

### **Budgeting**

* Each ship is treated as its **own financial entity**.
* ERP does **not** meet requirements for:
  * Daily fuel rates
  * Daily manning rates
* MSC has:
  * **Navy Working Capital Fund**
  * **TRANSCOM Working Capital Fund**

### **Automation Needs**

MSC wants increased automation for:

* Notifications
* System health before outages
* Reducing dependence on slow Navy PORs (SPS, EPS migration issues)

### **NOSS / Enterprise System Integration**

* MSC is operationally unique.
* Navy is not concerned about MSC-specific operational capabilities when pushing migrations.
* IBM informed of ongoing NOSS work; MSC expects integration by **FY30**.
